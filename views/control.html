Let's set up Live Translate

Two online services do the hard work. Each one gives you a key: a long password that lets this app use it. You copy the key from the service's website and paste it here. You only do this once.

👂DeepgramThe ears. Turns the speaker's voice into text, live.
🌍ClaudeThe translator. Turns that text into every other language.

You pay each service directly, only for what you use. New Deepgram accounts normally start with free credit. Your keys stay on this computer.

No internet at your events, or no keys? The app can also hear and translate on this laptop for free. Download the offline packs once in Settings → Offline mode. With keys and offline packs together, it uses the online services when it can and switches by itself when the internet drops.
Step 1 of 3 · the ears

Get your Deepgram key

  1. Click the button to open Deepgram's website.
  2. Create a free account (signing in with Google is fine).
  3. Open API Keys, press Create a New API Key, give it any name, and copy the key it shows.
  4. Come back here and paste it:

Step 2 of 3 · the translator

Get your Claude key

  1. Click the button to open the Claude developer website.
  2. Sign in or create an account. Under Billing, add a few dollars of credit (you only pay for what the translations use).
  3. Open API Keys, press Create Key, and copy the key.
  4. Come back here and paste it:

Step 3 of 3 · the microphone

Check your microphone

Press the button and say a few words. If the app hears you, the bars under it move and the Microphone box turns green.

Nothing moves? Choose the right microphone in the Microphone list on the Live page, and check it is not muted.

✓

You're ready

Choose the speaker's language, press Start listening, then Open big screen. Show the QR code so the audience can read on their phones.

Tip: download the offline packs (Settings → Offline mode) so the event keeps going if the internet fails.
